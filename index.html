    How it works

    Every car starts with the smallest possible brain: sensors and a GPS arrow wired straight to the controls. They grow neurons as they learn to stay on the road, then to reach a target, then to reach it through detours, darkness, weather and traffic. A new town arrives every six levels. Draw your own map and they'll learn that too.
